Fixed Cost Rates

Harvest storage defaults to 275 m³ — about a 28 × 28 ft cooler. Its weekly cost is the depreciation of the building (capital ÷ life ≈ $0.48/m³/wk), and the capacity also caps how much can be harvested in any week, so it is a cost and a constraint.
Weekly cost = (Capacity × $/m³) ÷ (Years × 52)
Irrigation cost is the depreciation of your installed system (capital ÷ useful life). Pumping energy sits in overhead. NOTE: well water itself is currently treated as free at the margin — the Water section caps the VOLUME available but does not price it — so there is no separate operating rate here to double-count. Fertilizer defaults to $0.82/lb.
Weekly irrigation cost = (Capital $/acre) ÷ (Years × 52)
Equipment-barn and fuel-tank costs are the depreciation of those assets (capital ÷ life). Machinery defaults to $1,000/wk (blended depreciation or loan payment + maintenance). Fuel is $1.25/L, metered per litre used. Enable to override.
Equip/fuel storage weekly cost = (Capital) ÷ (Years × 52)

ℹ Assumptions & Limitations
  • All cost rates (irrigation, equipment, machinery) are annual averages. Seasonal rate spikes are not modelled.
  • Budget is a single annual cash constraint. Owned assets (storage, irrigation, tanks, machinery) are charged as straight-line depreciation: capital cost ÷ (useful life × 52) per week.
  • Land is assumed to be flat, contiguous, and uniformly productive. Soil quality variation is not modelled.
  • Fixed infrastructure costs are incurred regardless of which crops are planted.

ℹ Assumptions & Limitations
  • Yields are fixed per-acre constants. Crop failure, weather damage, and pest pressure are not modelled (use yield_std_dev_pct in Monte Carlo inputs).
  • Growth cycles are rigid — phase durations do not vary with temperature, soil moisture, or planting date.
  • Successive plantings of the same crop are independent; soil depletion, disease carryover, and rotation benefits are not modelled.
  • Diversity constraint limits the annual acre-fraction any single crop can occupy; regulatory or buyer-imposed rotation requirements may differ.
  • Inventory carry IS modelled (since the inventory-core rewrite). Produce not sold in its harvest week is carried forward week by week, occupies storage for as long as it is held, and must be sold within the crop's shelf life or it is written off as waste — which the results report per crop. Storage is measured as everything in the room during the week: that week's harvest plus whatever was carried in. Produce sold the same week it is picked still counts, because field heat has to come off it either way, so the reported peak is a figure you can size a building from. Only culls are excluded — they are removed at harvest and never enter the room. Crops marked as not needing cold storage are held against a separate ambient (barn) capacity.

Constraint Utilization Summary

Unit Contribution Margins

Revenue minus all variable costs per acre, by crop and channel — this drives which crops the optimizer selects. Contribution Margin/ac = (yield/ac × price) − (packaging + labor + water + fertilizer + fuel + seed). Margin % of Rev = Contribution Margin ÷ Gross Revenue — a true gross margin percentage (negative values mean VC exceeds revenue). When Annualize for crop cycle length is on, the Annual Margin/ac column shows margin × (365 ÷ days to maturity) — useful for comparing fast-turnover crops (spinach) against slow ones (garlic).
